# Stereo-Mart v17 — art direction (audit → cartoon BT shop)

## Audit

### balmingtiger.com (room + brand)
- **Medium:** hand-drawn 2D equirect cartoon (not photo, not 3D).
- **Line:** dark organic outlines, slight wobble, readable silhouettes.
- **Light:** warm amber interior; night teal/blue outside windows.
- **Palette (sampled from BT og room):** cream/mustard floral wallpaper, warm wood, terracotta rug, soft ceiling glow, dark window panes.
- **Hotspots:** clean object edges so cream hover aura can sit on the rim only.
- **Feel:** cozy, premium, empty-enough to breathe — not a scrapbook collage.

### User style refs (attached)
1. **Indoor cartoon room** — bold ink, marker/pencil grain, saturated teal + orange + magenta, one-point depth, playful props with hard silhouettes.
2. **Coastal house** — Regular Show / indie TV cartoon: soft painterly fills, paper tooth, warm golden light, elevated readability.

### Current Stereo-Mart (v16)
- Muddy photoreal/composite, mean luma ~76 (BT room / prior v13 cartoon ~130+).
- Props look pasted; ink-flood edges failed; glow can’t look premium on soft photo edges.
- Too much poster noise; brand/hotspots don’t lead the eye.

## Target (v17)
- **Style:** user-ref cartoon craft (bold ink + painterly soft shade + light paper grain).
- **Color:** BT room warmth — cream damask walls, teal wainscot, amber wood, terracotta rug accents, night street through storefront, soft pendant glow.
- **Layout (keep UV anchors):**
  - CRT + cabinet @ storefront (authored u≈0.91)
  - Record bins island @ center (u≈0.49, v≈0.60)
  - LISTEN headphones + turntable @ back wall (u≈0.47, v≈0.36)
  - Cash register + rotary phone @ right counter (u≈0.22 / 0.15)
- **Premium rules:** clear negative space, no people, tight hero silhouettes, wrap-safe seam, zenith/nadir simple ceiling/floor.
