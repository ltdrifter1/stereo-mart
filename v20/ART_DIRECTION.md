# STEREO-MART v20 — Art Direction

## One-liner

You step into the middle of a small independent record label shop, fully
hand-illustrated, on a quiet overcast afternoon. Warm, weird, lived-in, full
of tiny discoveries. It should feel like a Hey Arnold! background you can
turn around inside — not a website.

## Reference collision

- balmingtiger.com — playful chaos, collage energy, in-world navigation
- Hey Arnold! city backgrounds — painted brick, telephone wires, mood
- Late-90s Nickelodeon environmental art — chunky shapes, character in props
- Cozy PNW record stores — wood floors, crates, rain-light through glass
- Seoul backstreets / Japanese neighborhood shops — signage, density, wires
- Underground electronic music culture + indie zine aesthetics — stickers,
  photocopy texture, hand lettering

## Hard rules

- Hand-drawn. Organic. Chunky outlines with varying weight.
- Visible brush / gouache texture. Nothing glossy, nothing gradient-smooth.
- Muted colours, cloudy PNW light. No pure black, no pure white.
- Slight imperfections everywhere: wobbly lines, uneven shelves, taped edges.
- NOT childish, NOT corporate, NOT photorealistic, NOT polished.
- Nothing in the frame may read as a photograph.

Lighting: single overcast key from the storefront window; warm practical
pools from desk lamp and listening station. Long soft shadows, no hard sun.

## Brand mark (from supplied logo)

Globe badge: cream disc, ink-navy latitude/longitude grid, bold italic
condensed "STEREO-MART" in navy across the equator, "RECORDS" in cream on a
muted-red banner beneath. Vector redraw lives in `brand/`. In-world, the mark
appears painted/aged: window decal (mirrored from inside), rubber stamps on
sleeves, a dusty neon-off sign, spine stickers — never a crisp digital logo.

## Interior inventory (the room)

Old wood floors · painted brick · uneven shelves · stacked vinyl · cassette
tape wall · posters layered over posters · listening station with headphones
· old CRT TV on a milk crate · boom box · houseplants · milk crates · vintage
speakers · coffee mugs · desk buried in demo tapes · half-open storage room
door · sleeping cat · old fan slowly rotating · big storefront window onto
the street.

## Exterior (through the glass)

Animated cartoon street: telephone wires, passing clouds, pigeons, a leaned
bicycle, streetlamp, apartment buildings, tiny ramen shop, corner
convenience store, small pedestrians. Everything gently alive.

## Motion language

Very subtle. Slow. Comfortable. Environmental loops only (Ghibli): cat
blink, fan rotation, coffee steam, cloud drift, wire sway, record spin.
No exaggerated squash-and-stretch, nothing abrupt.

## Plate specs

- Master: equirectangular 2:1, target 8192×4096 (paint at ≥4096×2048).
- Wrap seam lands on a quiet wall section (behind the viewer, storage side).
- Hero props live in the mid band (±35° pitch); zenith = ceiling w/ fan,
  nadir = floorboards w/ rug.
- Props/hotspot art export as transparent PNG (or SVG) overlays, painted in
  the same brush language so they disappear into the plate.
