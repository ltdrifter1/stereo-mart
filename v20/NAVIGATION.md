swell. Nothing abrupt.

## Ambient life (always-on loops)

cat blinks · fan rotates · record spins on the platter · speaker cone
vibrates faintly · desk lamp flickers once in a while · coffee steams ·
clouds pass outside · telephone wires sway · pigeons shuffle on the wire.

## Hidden discoveries (found by curiosity, tracked per-visit)

bird lands on window sill · poster corner lifts in a draft · cassette
rewinds by itself · vinyl sleeve slips slightly · tiny ghost appears in the
storage room for a second · rabbit sticker · turtle sticker · tiny mushroom
behind the plants · little smiley faces scratched into the desk · hidden
record label logos in poster art · CRT blips to a secret channel at a random
minute · cat stretches if you stare at it long enough.

Rule of thumb: a returning visitor should find something new every visit.

## Audio bed (opt-in, very quiet)

street murmur · birds · distant train · vinyl crackle · low HVAC hum ·
occasional muffled music from the back room. No autoplay music, ever.
